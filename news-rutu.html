Captain Ruturaj Gaikwad Leads The Preparations

Squad News

Captain Ruturaj Gaikwad is setting the tone for the upcoming season, leading from the front in the preparatory camps as the squad gears up for another thrilling campaign. Gaikwad's focus on fitness, technique, and team bonding has been evident during the rigorous training sessions in Chennai.

After taking over the reins, Gaikwad has shown exceptional maturity and a calm demeanor, traits highly valued by the CSK management. His meticulous preparation and dedication are rubbing off on the younger players in the squad.

The coaching staff, including head coach Stephen Fleming, have praised Gaikwad's proactive approach. Fans are eager to see how his leadership unfolds and if he can guide the team to yet another glorious IPL title.
