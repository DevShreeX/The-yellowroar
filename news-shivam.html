Shivam Dube Ready to Bring the Heat

Player Update

Power-hitter Shivam Dube has been honing his skills in the off-season and is fully prepared to provide the crucial middle-order acceleration the team needs. Dube's ability to take on spinners and clear the boundary with ease has made him an indispensable asset for CSK.

Reports from the training camp suggest that Dube is in prime form, consistently striking the ball clean and far. The coaching staff has been working closely with him to further refine his technique against high-pace bowling, making him a more complete and dangerous batter.

With his confidence sky-high, Dube is eager to step onto the field and entertain the yellow army. Fans can expect some fireworks when he comes out to bat this season.
