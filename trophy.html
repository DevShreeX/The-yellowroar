Our IPL Victories

Relive the glorious moments when the Yellow Army conquered the IPL.

🏆 1st Title

2010

The night the pride was born. Under the starry sky of Mumbai, MS Dhoni lifted our maiden trophy, proving that silence and strategy speak louder than words.

🏆 2nd Title

2011

A victory of sheer dominance. Chepauk roared as we became the first team to win back-to-back titles, stamping our authority as the undeniable Kings of the IPL.

🏆 3rd Title

2018

The fairytale comeback. After two years of tears and waiting, the 'Dad's Army' returned to show the world that age is just a number, but loyalty is forever.

🏆 4th Title

2021

Rising from the ashes. Written off by many after a tough previous season, Thala's men silenced the critics in Dubai with a magical, flawless campaign.

🏆 5th Title

2023

A climax written in the stars. In the pouring rain of Ahmedabad, Jaddu's final-ball boundary gave MS Dhoni the perfect tribute, sealing our legendary fifth star.
