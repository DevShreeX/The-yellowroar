Nurturing the Future: Junior Super Kings

Discover how the Chennai Super Kings are building the next generation of cricket stars.

The Junior Super Kings is a premier inter-school T20 cricket tournament organized by the Chennai Super Kings. Launched in 2012, this incredible initiative aims to identify and nurture young, promising cricketing talent right at the grassroots level.

Expanding Horizons

What started as a focused tournament in Tamil Nadu has now grown tremendously. The recent 2024-25 edition witnessed participation from over 100 schools across 23 districts in Tamil Nadu, and even included teams from Andhra Pradesh, Goa, Karnataka, and Kerala. The ultimate vision is to transform this into an all-India tournament.

A Proven Track Record

The tournament serves as a highly competitive platform for young athletes to showcase their skills under professional conditions. It is recognized by the Tamil Nadu Cricket Association (TNCA) and has been instrumental in scouting future stars.

Stars Discovered Through Junior Super Kings:

  • Washington Sundar - Star Indian All-rounder
  • Shahrukh Khan - Explosive Middle-order Batter
  • Sai Sudharsan - Elegant Top-order Batter
  • Hari Nishanth - Opening Batter

🎯 The Vision

Our vision is to provide a world-class platform where school-level cricketers can experience the pressure and glory of professional T20 cricket. We aim to unearth raw talent from rural and urban areas alike, providing them with the necessary exposure and guidance.

🏆 Format & Rules

The tournament typically features a multi-phase format. Phase 1 includes district-level knockouts and league matches. The winners advance to the grand Phase 2, held in premier venues, culminating in a high-stakes final under floodlights with colored clothing and white balls.

🚀 Career Launchpad

Top performers are awarded exclusive opportunities, including specialized coaching clinics, scholarships, and potential entry into the official CSK Academy. It's not just a tournament; it's a stepping stone to the IPL and national squads.
