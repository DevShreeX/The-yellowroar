Exclusive MS Dhoni Content on CSK TV

CSK TV

The iconic MS Dhoni continues to be the heartbeat of the Chennai Super Kings. Fans can now catch the latest behind-the-scenes action and exclusive features showcasing the legendary 'Thala' on CSK TV.

The new segments include 'Care for Kings', which highlights untold stories and memorable interactions between Dhoni and the support staff over the years. Additionally, the platform is releasing never-before-seen moments from training sessions, locker room speeches, and light-hearted off-field banter.

This initiative aims to bring the fans closer to their beloved captain and provide a deeper glimpse into his unparalleled legacy and the profound impact he has had on the franchise and the sport as a whole.
